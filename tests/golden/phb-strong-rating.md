ing. To fight on, a force must have at least 1 left in the Defense or Attack Rating. If the battle continues, roll again as per above, turn after turn, until one side surrenders or is completely defeated (its Defense or Attack Rating hits zero).

##### Losses

Each rolled ⚔ means losses on the opposing side. The side suffering the losses gets to decide what each lost point of Defense or Attack Rating means:

The defenders of the stronghold can choose between these losses:

- Ten GUARDS in the stronghold are Broken. Roll for critical injuries as needed.
- The RAMPARTS of the stronghold are damaged and only give +1 to the Defense Rating.
- Damaged RAMPARTS are breached and give no more bonuses to the Defense Rating.

The raw materials remain and can be used to rebuild the RAMPARTS.

- The PORTCULLIS of the stronghold is destroyed. The raw materials remain and can be used to rebuild it.
